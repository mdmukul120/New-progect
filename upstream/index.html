About Hamyra

Hamyra is a free streaming and download hub for Bangladeshi and global audiences. Watch Hollywood and Bollywood movies, Bangla natok, Korean drama, web series and anime in HD — plus 100+ live TV channels and live cricket — all in your browser, with no signup and no app to install.

The movie and series catalog spans trending releases, award-winning films and full seasons of popular shows, many with Hindi-dubbed and dual-audio tracks and subtitles. Fresh Bollywood, Hindi-dubbed and regional titles arrive fast through our HamyraBD collection, while the downloads library carries 36,000+ movies, dramas and web series in 480p, 720p and 1080p — including Chorki, Hoichoi and KLiKK originals.

Live TV brings Bangladeshi channels like T Sports and BTV alongside international news and sports networks, and match days add live cricket and football. Everything streams instantly in a clean, ad-light player that works on phones, tablets and desktops. Browse by category, search any title, and pick up right where you left off with Continue Watching.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hamyra free to use?

Yes. Hamyra is completely free — you can watch movies, Bangla natok, web series, live TV and live cricket, and download titles, without paying or creating an account.

Do I need to sign up or install an app?

No signup is needed and everything plays in your browser on phone or desktop. A free Android app is also available from the Get the App page if you prefer.

Hollywood, Bollywood and Bangla movies, Bangla natok, Korean and Chinese drama, anime, web series from Chorki, Hoichoi and KLiKK, plus 100+ live TV channels and live cricket.

Can I download movies and web series?

Yes. Most titles offer direct download links in 480p, 720p, 1080p and 4K, including dual-audio and Hindi-dubbed releases, and the catalog is updated daily.

Yes. Hamyra is built for Bangladeshi viewers with Bangla movies, natok and local TV channels, and it works worldwide in any modern browser.
